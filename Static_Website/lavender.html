Discover White Oak Lavender Farm!

White Oak Lavender Farm is known for its beautiful lavender fields, relaxing atmosphere, and variety of lavender-based products. It's a popular destination for visitors who enjoy nature, photography, shopping, and outdoor activities.

Why visit White Oak Lavender Farm?

  • Explore the beautiful lavender gardens
  • Shop for lavender-based products, like soap, hand cream, and lavender sugar
  • Visit the Purple WOLF Vineyard
  • Enjoy outdoor activities
