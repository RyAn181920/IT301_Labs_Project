Experience Virginia's First Buc-ee's!

Located in Mount Crawford, just outside of Harrisonburg, Buc-ee's is a massive gas station known for its wide selection of snacks, souvenir's, and delicious freshly prepared food. It opened in June 2025 as Virginia's first Buc'ees, and has quickly become one of the most popular tourist spots off route 81.
