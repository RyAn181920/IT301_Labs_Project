James Madison University

Discover JMU

Located in the heart of Harrisonburg, Virginia, James Madison University is a public university known for its beautiful campus, academic opportunities, and students with dedicated school spirit. JMU isn't just a school, it's a tourist attraction as well! Visitors can admire the beautiful campus views and historic buildings, or maybe watch a football game and get involved in the high energy of the crowd! Whether you're a prospective student, a family member, or simply exploring Harrisonburg, JMU is a great place to visit.
