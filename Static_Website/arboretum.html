Explore the Beauty of Nature!

The Edith J. Carrier Arboretum is a beautiful nature preserve located on James Madison University's campus in Harrisonburg, Virginia. Featuring scenic walking trails, peaceful gardens, and a variety of native plants, the arboretum offers visitors a relaxing escape into nature. Whether you're interested in photography, hiking, or simply enjoying the outdoors, the arboretum is a great destination to explore.

Why Visit the Arboretum?

  • Explore scenic walking trails
  • Enjoy beautiful gardens and native plants
  • Take photographs of nature and wildlife
  • Relax and enjoy the peaceful environment

Learn more about the arboretum by visiting the official JMU Arboretum website.
